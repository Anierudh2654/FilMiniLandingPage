01   What's in it for Directors?

Where stories
find their faces.

Stories are written, Screenplays are drafted, but it is not documents that reach user screens. FilMini offers a platform to connect with a wide range of talented filmmakers, who can contribute on and off screen. Directors can choose people to work with by viewing their previous work and can be selective to match the descriptions in their mind. What a shift from asking friends if they could act!

Public Auditions  ·  Scroll Content  ·  Networking

02   Storage and Documentation

Worried
about losing your work?

Where closing files without saving is a thing of the past. Our secure cloud storage ensures your work is always safe and accessible. Save your story and screenplay drafts, grant co-authors access to edit, store video clips scene-by-scene alongside editor notes. Now you don't need to fear losing a hard drive!

Story Screenplay Editor  ·  Clips Page  ·  Co-Authors

03   What's in it for Actors?

REACH OUT
to directors from your abode.

Actors can showcase their talent and connect with directors directly through our platform. Post content to your profile, mention collaborators in each post, participate in open auditions, and get discovered by casting directors who are looking for the perfect fit for their projects.

Open Auditions  ·  Media Gallery  ·  Collaborators

04   The Application

An application that
artists and the art itself appreciate.

This application is built by a team that admires movies, respects the craft, and believes in the power of storytelling. This app is a reminder that art is the only innate human skill that can't be replaced by machines. And we promise: there's much more to come.

The FilMini Manifesto

Film is not created alone —
it lives in directors who see stories in strangers,
actors who dare to go beyond themselves,
and artists whose craft defies imitation.
FilMini: where every story finds its makers.

FilMini exists for the ones who believe in the work before the applause. For the director who casts with instinct. For the actor who prepares in silence. For the writer who rewrites at midnight. This platform was built for you — and for everyone you haven't met yet.
